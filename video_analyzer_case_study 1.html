Video Analyzer: Secure Multi-Tenant Video Analytics Platform

Multi-tenant SaaS platform for AI-powered video analytics, supporting live CCTV/RTSP streams, zones, rules, intrusion, loitering, occupancy, and automated PDF/Excel reports.

Executive Summary

The Video Analyzer platform converts raw camera streams and historical video/image uploads into direct operational insights. Scoped strictly under tenant isolation, the system uses YOLO26 + ByteTrack and deep face recognition to enable real-time detection, loitering alerts, zone occupancy counting, and rapid face searches across indexed database collections.

The Operational Problem Space

CCTV Surveillance Data Vacuum

Over 95% of corporate video feeds remain completely unreviewed, resulting in petabytes of expensive spatial data and visitor records being lost, while manual face matching or event tracking takes hours of forensic scanning.

High Friction Event & Acknowledgment Workflows

Sifting through raw streams manually to track occupancy limits, loitering infractions, or restricted zone intrusions incurs high human error rates, operational fatigue, and delayed security response latencies.

Absent Multi-Tenant Isolation & Privacy Controls

Traditional physical locations lack structured visitor flow analytics, while legacy analytics systems lack tenant-level database isolation, role-based guardrails, and secure API-driven audit logs.

Strategic Engineering Decisions

Compute & Continuity Obstacles

Intense Real-Time Inference Costs

Executing deep computer vision models frame-by-frame on high-resolution, high-FPS video uploads triggers high CPU/GPU compute costs and bottlenecks background task execution.

Path Discontinuity Across Streams

Standard object tracking algorithms assign new tracker IDs to the same target if bounding boxes overlap poorly or if targets exit the frame briefly, producing fragmented tracking records.

Security Auditing Stage: JWT / RBAC Active

Engineered Solutions

Downscaling & Keyframe Inference

Implemented dynamic frame step sampling (interval-based skipping) and automatic image downscaling (640px max dimension limit) inside Celery task workers to optimize inference cycles.

PostgreSQL + pgvector Indexing

Built a unified face tracking catalog storing 512-dimension visual features natively in PostgreSQL with pgvector, allowing sub-second cosine distance searches scoped to each tenant.

Database: PostgreSQL + pgvector + Redis Cache
